Independent Structural Model Verification — Structural Engineering Services

Independent Verification of Structural Models

Independent reproduction of the results of your structural model using the direct stiffness method and OpenSees, to cross-check displacements, internal forces and reactions obtained with commercial software (ETABS, SAP2000 or others). Discrepancies are identified and quantified, their probable origin is documented (modeling assumptions, boundary conditions, loads or load combinations), and a technical judgment on their relevance is issued, with full traceability between the mathematical model and the results.

The method is publicly documented: a 21-member plane truss solved with a custom stiffness-method implementation and verified against OpenSeesPy, with exact agreement across 84 values (DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.20042213).

Independent Technical Review of Drawings and Calculation Reports

Technical review of the structural design, calculation reports and detailing drawings prepared by the client or by third parties, checking compliance with the applicable code (ACI 318, ASCE 7, NEC-15 or the code specified by the client). The review covers loads, load combinations, modeling, analysis results and consistency between calculation report and drawings. The deliverable is a document of classified, traceable observations and technical judgment, addressed to the client so that their engineer of record can evaluate and incorporate them.

Seismic Analysis

Determination of seismic demand and verification of structural performance using the equivalent lateral force procedure of ASCE 7 or the code specified by the client, including the seismic coefficient, base shear, vertical distribution of forces and torsional effects. Reports include story drifts, internal forces and irregularity checks, as technical support for the decisions of the engineer of record.

How It Works

  • 1. Send your model — ETABS (.e2k), SAP2000 (.s2k) or OpenSees files, plus the governing code.
  • 2. Fixed-price quote — scope, delivery time and price agreed before any work starts.
  • 3. Verification report — discrepancy tables, comparison plots and technical judgment, delivered in English or Spanish.

All communication is written, which keeps every decision documented and traceable.

Scope of services. Review, verification and analysis services consist of observations and technical judgment addressed exclusively to the client; authorship, signature and responsibility for any documents the client prepares or submits rest with the client's engineer of record. No filings, procedures or submissions are made before public authorities on behalf of third parties.
